# Sale contract (Contract de vânzare-cumpărare)

## Functionality

This feature fills in and generates the official Romanian sale contract for a
vehicle (ITL 054, Anexa nr. 2 — "contract de înstrăinare-dobândire a unui
mijloc de transport") as a ready-to-print PDF, and keeps a record of it.

### Creating a contract — the wizard

Reached via "New contract" from the contract list. A 5-step wizard:

1. **Role** — are you selling or buying this car? This decides which of the
   two sections on the printed form ("Your details") your own information
   fills, and which one ("The other party's details") the counterparty's
   information fills.
2. **Vehicle** — the car's details (make, type, VIN, plate, engine, weight,
   CIV number, manufacture year, Euro norm, ITP expiry, how/when it was
   acquired). Three ways to fill it in, freely switchable:
   - **From your garage** — pick one of your own cars and its data pre-fills.
   - **Photograph the registration certificate** — a photo or PDF is read and
     the fields are suggested.
   - **Type it manually.**
   Whichever route you take lands on the same editable form — nothing is
   ever auto-accepted, you always see and can correct every field.
3. **Parties** — two sections, "Your details" and "The other party's
   details" (seller/buyer, per the role you picked in step 1):
   - **Your details** — a normal form (name or company name, CNP/CIF, ID
     series and number, address, phone, email; a company additionally has a
     legal representative and an optional separate fiscal address). If you
     saved your identity on a previous contract, it's pre-filled
     automatically. Above the form, a "Photograph the identity document"
     button lets you scan or upload your own ID (photo or PDF) instead of
     typing it — the fields are read and pre-filled the same way the vehicle
     step reads a registration certificate; you can still edit anything
     afterward. A checkbox lets you save your details again for next time.
   - **The other party's details** — same form, but starting empty and with
     an explicit first choice between "Photograph the identity document" and
     "Fill in manually". Choosing to scan first asks for a one-time consent
     confirmation, since it's someone else's document, not something you can
     agree to on their behalf: a checkbox stating the other person agrees to
     have their ID photographed and its data used for this contract.
     Declining is a normal path, not an error — it simply switches to typing.
     Once accepted, scanning works the same as everywhere else: a suggestion,
     shown with its confidence and any warnings, that you review before it's
     final (for example, a CNP that fails its checksum is still shown, not
     silently dropped, flagged so you can compare it against the document and
     fix a misread digit).
4. **Price and signing** — price in lei (the amount in words is generated by
   the backend from the figure, not entered separately), signing date,
   signing place, and whether the contract has annexes.
5. **Review and generate** — a read-only summary of everything entered, the
   retention choice (see below), and four confirmation checkboxes that must
   all be ticked before the "Generate" button is enabled. None of them are
   pre-ticked and there is no "tick all" — each corresponds to one of the
   declarations already printed on section (5) of the official form
   (verifying the data is correct, accepting the declarations, acknowledging
   the criminal-code warning, acknowledging the disclaimer). Generating
   produces the PDF, downloads it, and opens the contract's detail page.

You can leave the wizard at any point via the close button in the top bar;
if you already have unsaved or saved-but-not-generated changes, it asks for
confirmation first. The top-bar save button stores the current state as a
draft on the server and lets you keep working later — there is no
on-device/offline draft, since the form can hold a third party's personal
data.

### Retention

Every generated contract keeps its personal data (names, CNPs, addresses, ID
numbers) for 90 days by default — long enough to print/file it — after which
it's automatically erased. On the review step, ticking "keep in this car's
history" extends that to 5 years instead, for contracts you want to keep
showing up in the car's own history. Either way, once the window closes, the
contract itself is not deleted: it's marked "purged", and what survives is
non-identifying — the vehicle, the price, the date — so the history doesn't
develop a hole. A purged contract can no longer be edited or downloaded.

### The contract list and detail view

- **List** — every contract you've created, showing role, make, plate,
  price, signing date and status (Draft / Generated / Purged). Actions per
  row: open, continue editing a draft, download the PDF (generated
  contracts only), delete.
- **Detail** — the full read-only record: vehicle, both parties' sections
  (blank once purged), price/signing info, retention date, and which of the
  four confirmations were ticked when it was generated. Actions: edit,
  delete, download.

## Implementation

**Frontend** — `history-auto-utility-fe/src/app/features/sale-contract/`
- `sale-contract-wizard/` — the 5-step create/continue-draft form (`new` and
  `:id/edit` both route here). `sale-contract-wizard.component.ts` owns all
  wizard state; see its class-level doc comment for the three non-obvious
  design points (consent gates the camera for the other party only,
  extraction is always a suggestion never auto-accepted, the four
  confirmations are a first-class gate).
- `sale-contract-list/` — list page, `SaleContractRowVm` built only from
  `SaleContractSummary` (the list endpoint never decrypts party data).
- `sale-contract-detail/` — read-only detail page; the only place a loaded
  `SaleContract` (with decrypted parties) is held — no store, no cache.
- `components/contract-vehicle-form/` — the vehicle step's form, reused as-is
  regardless of source (garage/scan/manual).
- `components/contract-party-form/` (`ContractPartyFormComponent`) — one
  party's fields (section (1) or (2) of the paper form); used for both "me"
  and "other", switching label set (person vs. company) off `is_company`.
- `components/contract-address-form/` — one address's blanks (street/
  building/locality breakdown), used for a party's home and fiscal address.
- `components/identity-consent/` (`IdentityConsentComponent`) — the
  third-party consent gate; only ever shown in front of the **other**
  party's scan picker, never the user's own (see below).
- `components/extraction-review/` (`ExtractionReviewComponent`) — renders
  confidence + warnings above whichever form the extraction filled.
- `components/wizard-steps/` — the step indicator/breadcrumb at the top of
  the wizard.
- `sale-contract.model.ts` — hand-written types for the API (not generated —
  see the file's own doc comment on why `@hau/autogenapi` isn't used here).
- `sale-contract.routes.const.ts` — route paths, and
  `SALE_CONTRACT_CONSENT_VERSION` (the version of the consent wording sent
  to the backend; **bump it whenever the consent text changes**, or old
  consent records misrepresent what was actually shown).
- API access: `history-auto-utility-fe/src/app/core/sale-contract-api.service.ts`
  (plain `HttpClient`, not the generated client, matching the model file).

**Backend** — `history-auto-utility-be/src/modules/sale-contract/`
- `sale-contract.controller.ts` — `POST /sale-contract/extract-identity`
  (stateless — the photo is processed in memory and discarded, nothing is
  persisted by this call; requires `consent_version` unconditionally, since
  the backend has no way to know whose document it is), `GET`/`PUT`/`DELETE
  /sale-contract/my-identity` (the logged-in user's own saved identity,
  reused to pre-fill "Your details" on future contracts), plus standard
  list/create/get/update/`:id/generate`/`:id/download`/delete on
  `/sale-contract`.
- `sale-contract.service.ts` — CRUD, `consent_version`/`consent_accepted_at`
  bookkeeping (only meaningful for the other party's data — see the model's
  doc comment on `CreateSaleContractPayload.consent_version`), retention
  deadline calculation (90 days default / 5 years with `keep_in_history`).
- `sale-contract-retention.service.ts` — daily cron (03:15) that erases
  `SaleContractParty.data_encrypted` and the stored PDF once
  `retention_until` passes, marking the row `PURGED` rather than deleting it.
- `pdf/sale-contract-pdf.service.ts`, `pdf/price-in-words.util.ts` — PDF
  rendering and the Romanian price-in-words conversion.
- `extraction/identity-extraction.service.ts` — the OCR/extraction provider
  behind `extract-identity`.
- Party rows (`SaleContractParty`, one SELLER + one BUYER per contract) store
  `data_encrypted` — encrypted at rest via `FieldEncryptionService`.

### Own-identity scan vs. other-party scan (2026-09-24)

Identity-document scanning exists for **both** parties, but the two paths
are deliberately asymmetric:

- **The other party's section** gates the camera behind
  `IdentityConsentComponent`: the document usually belongs to someone who
  isn't a user of this app, so an explicit "this person agreed" checkbox has
  to be ticked before the picker is reachable, and the version of that
  wording is what gets sent to the backend. Declining just switches to
  typing. State: `otherPartySource`, `consentVisible`, `consentAccepted`,
  `identityExtracting`/`identityExtraction`/`identityExtractionFailed`/
  `identityExtractionUnavailable`, handled by `selectOtherPartySource()`,
  `onConsentAccepted()`/`onConsentDeclined()`, `onIdentityFileSelected()`.
- **Your own section** ("me") has no consent screen — you're the one
  photographing your own document, so there's no third party who needs to
  agree. It's a plain "Photograph the identity document" button sitting
  above the always-visible party form (not gated behind a scan/manual
  choice, unlike the other party's section — the form has to stay visible
  regardless, since it may already be pre-filled from a saved identity).
  State: `myIdentityExtracting`/`myIdentityExtraction`/
  `myIdentityExtractionFailed`/`myIdentityExtractionUnavailable`, handled by
  `onMyIdentityFileSelected()`. It still calls the same
  `POST /sale-contract/extract-identity` endpoint with
  `SALE_CONTRACT_CONSENT_VERSION`, because the backend requires a non-empty
  `consent_version` on every call regardless of whose document it is — that
  field is just being satisfied, not a real consent record for your own
  data. Both paths share `_applyIdentityExtraction(group, result)`, which
  patches whichever `FormGroup` (`meGroup` or `otherGroup`) is passed in.

Before this, only the other party's section had a scan option at all — your
own identity could only be typed in or pulled from a previously saved
identity. This was reported as "the buyer can't scan their ID" because
whichever role you pick, **your own** section ("me") never had it: picking
the buyer role made the seller (the other party) the one with a working scan
option, and vice versa. Fixed by adding the scan affordance to "me" as
described above, rather than by changing the role/section mapping, which was
correct — "the other party" always means the one whose document is not
provably yours.

### Vehicle scan field coverage (2026-09-24)

The "Photograph the registration certificate" vehicle-step scan goes through
the same shared, generic extraction endpoint the Documents feature and
`cars-form` use (`POST /api/document/extract`,
`history-auto-utility-be/src/modules/document/`), not a sale-contract-owned
one — see `onRegistrationFileSelected()`'s doc comment. That endpoint's
Gemini prompt/schema (`document/gemini-extraction.service.ts`,
`ExtractedFieldsDto`) is shared across every document type it recognises, so
adding a contract-only field means extending that shared schema, not just the
wizard.

`_applyVehicleExtraction()` only maps across the subset of
`ExtractedFieldsDto` that the contract's vehicle form actually has a blank
for (`make`, `type`, `vin`, `license_plate`, `manufacture_year`,
`engine_capacity_cm3`, `max_weight_tons`, `itp_expiry_date`, `civ_number`).
Until 2026-09-24, `civ_number` ("seria și numărul cărții de identitate a
vehiculului", C.I.V.) wasn't in the shared schema at all — the field existed
on the contract form but the registration-certificate scan could never fill
it, so it always had to be typed in by hand regardless of source. Fixed by
adding `civ_number` to the Gemini prompt/schema and `ExtractedFieldsDto`
(backend and the hand-maintained frontend copy in
`auto-gen/api/models/extraction-result-dto.ts` — see the model's own doc
comment on why this client isn't regenerated from the spec) and mapping it in
`_applyVehicleExtraction()`.

**`engine_series`, `euro_norm`, `acquired_date` and `acquired_document`
have the same gap** — they're blanks on the contract's vehicle form with no
corresponding field in the shared extraction schema, so a registration-
certificate scan can never fill them either; they're typed in by hand today.
Not fixed as part of the above, since only the CIV number was reported.

### Known limitations

- The other party's consent checkbox is a self-report, not a signature —
  there is no way to verify the other person actually agreed, only that the
  wording was shown before the picker unlocked.
- Draft persistence is server-only by design (no local/offline draft), so a
  contract in progress needs connectivity to save.
