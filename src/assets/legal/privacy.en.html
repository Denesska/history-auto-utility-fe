3. Source of the data

We collect personal data:

  • directly from the User — any information entered or uploaded into the App;
  • from Google Sign-In — email, name, profile picture, Google ID, upon sign-in;
  • from the Organization you belong to — if you hold an organization-managed account, the Organization creates your account and may enter data about you (e.g. driver assignments);
  • from other users — for example, when another user invites you to share a vehicle, or enters your data as a party in a sale-purchase contract.

4. Is providing the data mandatory?

Your email address (obtained through Google sign-in or, for organization-managed accounts, your username) is required to create an account — without it we cannot provide the Service. Other data is provided optionally, depending on the features you choose to use; not providing it may limit the availability of certain features (for example, expiry reminders require entering the document's data).

6. Automated processing through artificial intelligence

To make data entry easier, the App may send:

  • images of vehicle documents (insurance policy, ITP, etc.) to Google (Gemini API), for automatic extraction of relevant fields (e.g. policy number, validity period);
  • the ID card scan, within the contract generator, to Anthropic (Claude API), for automatic extraction of the party's identification data. The image is not stored by the App, and the extracted values are not logged.

The result of this automatic extraction may occasionally be incorrect or incomplete — the User must check and, if necessary, correct the data before relying on it.

The App does not use automated decision-making with legal effects on you and does not perform profiling within the meaning of Art. 22 GDPR.

7. Processing of the national ID number (CNP)

The Romanian national ID number (CNP) is processed by the App only when the User voluntarily enters it, as part of a document (e.g. an RCA insurance policy) or the sale-purchase contract generated in the App. This processing is carried out under Art. 4 of Law no. 190/2018 on measures implementing the GDPR, solely for the purpose for which the User entered it (recording that document or generating the contract), and is subject to the security measures described in Section 11 (including encryption of contract data).

8. Business accounts — the Organization as controller

When an Organization enters into the App personal data about its own employees or drivers (for example, driver assignments and their history), the Organization is the controller of that data, and DENHAU acts as a processor, under the data processing clause in the Terms and Conditions (Section 5.1). For questions about data processed about you in this capacity, please contact the Organization you belong to.

9. Data retention

  • Account data is kept for as long as the account is active.
  • Upon account deletion: personal data is deleted immediately, the user record is anonymized, files are deleted from storage, and backups still containing them are rotated out within 30 days.
  • Data entered by an Organization remains with the Organization, subject to its own policies.
  • Sale-purchase contract party data is automatically deleted 90 days after generation by default, or after up to 5 years if the User explicitly chose that duration.
  • Server logs are kept for up to 30 days.
  • Proof of acceptance of the Terms and Privacy Policy is kept for as long as the account exists.
  • Notifications and activity statistics are kept for as long as the account exists.

10. Your rights

Regarding your personal data, you have the right to request: access to your data, rectification of inaccurate data, erasure of data, restriction of processing, data portability, objection to processing based on legitimate interest, and withdrawal of consent where processing is based on consent (without affecting the lawfulness of processing carried out before withdrawal).

You can exercise these rights directly from the App:

  • Settings → Account → "Download my data" — export of your data in JSON format;
  • Settings → Account → "Delete my account".

If you use an organization-managed account, some of these requests must be addressed to your Organization, which is the controller of that data.

You may also send a request to office@denhau.ro; we will respond within one month at most.

You have the right to lodge a complaint with the Romanian National Supervisory Authority for Personal Data Processing (ANSPDCP), located at B-dul G-ral. Gheorghe Magheru no. 28-30, Sector 1, Bucharest, www.dataprotection.ro.

11. Data security

We apply technical and organizational measures to protect your data, including: encrypted HTTPS connections, encryption of sensitive identity data (AES-256-GCM for contract data), access controls, httpOnly authentication cookies, and passwords stored using hashing functions (bcrypt).

12. Device storage (cookies and similar technologies)

The App uses only storage that is strictly necessary for it to function:

  • httpOnly authentication cookies (access token and refresh token);
  • localStorage: selected language, theme (light/dark), view mode, last sign-in tab used, remembered business username (only if you check "remember me"), various UI hints;
  • sessionStorage: navigation history within the App.

We do not use analytics or advertising modules and we do not place marketing trackers. The fonts used by the App are served from our own servers, not third-party services.

Because we use only storage strictly necessary for the Service to function, the App does not display a cookie consent banner, in accordance with the exemption under Art. 4(5) of Law no. 506/2004 on the processing of personal data in the electronic communications sector.

13. Minors

The Service is intended only for persons who are at least 18 years old. We do not knowingly collect data from minors. If you have reason to believe a minor has provided us with personal data, please contact us at office@denhau.ro.
